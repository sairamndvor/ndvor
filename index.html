Our Engineering & Modernization Approach

ndVOR follows a proven 4-stage modernization methodology:

  • 1. Discovery & Code Audit: Complete architectural assessment of existing codebases and data dependencies.
  • 2. Microservice Decoupling: Isolating core business logic into cloud-native APIs without disrupting daily operations.
  • 3. Parallel Execution & Testing: Running new cloud modules side-by-side with legacy systems to verify performance.
  • 4. Seamless Cutover & 24/7 Managed Operations: Zero-downtime deployment backed by automated failover.
