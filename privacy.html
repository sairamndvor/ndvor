Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

ndVOR Solutions LLC ("ndVOR", "we", "us") collects business contact information provided voluntarily through our website, consultation forms, and client engagements. This may include names, work email addresses, job titles, company details, and project inquiry details.

2. How We Use Enterprise Information

Information collected is strictly utilized to evaluate enterprise requirements, provide architecture recommendations, execute service contracts, and maintain secure business communications. We do not sell, license, or lease client information to third parties.

3. Data Security & Encryption

All data transmitted through ndVOR platforms is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 standards. Our operational procedures conform to SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA data protection guidelines.

4. Data Processing & Subprocessors

For client software engagements, data processing activities are governed by formal Data Processing Addendums (DPAs). Approved infrastructure subprocessors (e.g., AWS, Microsoft Azure) adhere to SOC 2 and GDPR compliance frameworks.

5. Contacting Our Data Protection Officer

For questions regarding privacy practices or to submit data subject access requests, contact our privacy team at privacy@ndvor.com or security@ndvor.com.
